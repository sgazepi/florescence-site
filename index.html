Why we're doing this

A note from Celia

This year, a health setback had me working with several specialists while also chasing my career goals. I found some wonderful doctors along the way, but until then it often fell to me to connect the dots: making sure my other risk factors were considered, weighing advice that didn't always agree, and making big decisions without a clear picture of how my body was doing day to day, or anyone with the same mix of conditions to compare notes with. I realized that this is the ongoing reality for many people living with chronic conditions. I'd like to take some of that load off, and to do it right, I want to hear from you first. If you're open to sharing your experience, I'd be truly grateful.

Celia, founder
